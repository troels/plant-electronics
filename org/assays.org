#+TITLE: Assays: how each compound is measured
#+STARTUP: overview

Back to [[file:index.org][index]]. Compounds are described in [[file:targets.org][targets]]; supplies are in the [[file:shopping.org::#assay][shopping list]].

Procedure details here are starting points from general knowledge unless
a source is named in [[file:literature.org][literature]]. Each one has to be tuned and checked on
your own plants before its numbers are trusted.

* Redness by camera
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: camera
:END:

1. Capture under the fixed white light with a grey card in frame ([[file:rig.org::#camera-protocol][protocol]]).
2. Normalise the image to the grey card.
3. Mask plant pixels.
4. Compute (red - green) / (red + green) per pixel; report the median over the plant and the red-area fraction.
5. Report projected leaf area as the growth measure.

*Validation:* at harvest, extract pigment from leaf discs and compare
with the camera index across at least 20 discs spanning pale to dark.

** Pigment extract (validation only)

- Leaf disc in acidified ethanol, in the dark, until the disc is pale.
- Measure absorbance in green light (about 530 nm) with a simple photometer.
- This gives relative pigment per disc area, which is all the validation needs.

* Vitamin C by voltammetry
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: vitc
:END:

1. Sample at the same hour each time, the same leaf position on each plant.
2. Weigh about 0.5 g of fresh leaf.
3. Grind in 2 mL of cold dilute oxalic acid (citric acid as a household fallback; metaphosphoric acid is the textbook choice but is only sold to businesses).
4. Let it settle or filter; put a drop on a carbon electrode with buffer.
5. Sweep the voltage upward from 0 V and record the oxidation peak.
6. Add a known amount of vitamin C to the same extract and repeat (standard addition). The step in peak height calibrates that sample.
7. Finish within minutes of grinding.

*Checks:*
- *Specificity:* measure an aliquot again after its vitamin C has been destroyed by leaving it open and warm. What remains is interference from other compounds. The enzyme ascorbate oxidase does this more cleanly, but is only sold to businesses.
- *Cross-check:* an iodine titration on the same extract. Test strips start at 50 mg/L, which is probably above what a lettuce extract holds.
- *Repeatability:* three samples from one plant should agree within about 15%.

*Standard:* pure vitamin C powder, dissolved fresh each session.

* Nicotine by voltammetry
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: nicotine-assay
:END:

1. Punch a disc from a leaf of defined position and age.
2. Extract in buffer.
3. Sweep to about +1.2 V and record the peak. Its position depends on the electrode: published values run from +0.6 to +1.3 V.
4. Standard addition with a nicotine solution of known strength.

*Checks:*
- *Interference:* a published attempt in a food mixture recovered only 27-34% of added nicotine. Always use standard addition in the leaf extract itself, and report relative change against day 0 of the same plant.
- *Assay validation:* a wounded plant must read higher than its unwounded neighbour after about five days. If the assay cannot see that, it cannot judge the electrical treatments.
- *Cross-check:* send a few paired samples to a lab with chromatography.

* Electrodes and instrument

- *Instrument:* a potentiostat controlled from the host ([[file:shopping.org::#potentiostat][options]]).
- *Electrodes:* disposable screen-printed carbon, one per sample, or home-made pencil-lead electrodes for development work.
- *Records:* every sweep is stored raw with its sample ID, so peak-finding can be redone later.

* Quality rules for all assays

- Every session includes a blank and a standard.
- Samples are coded so the person measuring does not know the treatment.
- Results enter the same database as the sensor data, linked to plant, zone and batch.

* Safety
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: safety
:END:

- *Nicotine:* it passes through skin from wet leaves, so wear gloves for tobacco work. A leaf punch holds tens of micrograms (my estimate); a reanalysis puts the lower limit for a fatal dose at 0.5-1 g swallowed. Do not concentrate extracts.
- *Nicotine standard:* the stock solution is the most concentrated nicotine on the bench. Gloves, a labelled closed bottle, out of reach of children and pets.
- *Acids and ethanol:* dilute and low-hazard, but wear eye protection when grinding.
