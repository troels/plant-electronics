How to use this file:
- Each item is a heading. Change =BUY= to =ORDERED=, =HAVE= or =SKIP= as you go.
- =EST= is the estimated cost in US dollars for the whole quantity of the recommended pick, before shipping.
- =PRICE= says whether that figure rests on a store price seen on 2026-11-22 (=checked=) or is still my guess (=memory=).
- =C-c C-x C-c= on a section shows it as a table with the costs summed.
- Tags give the stage that first needs the item (=s0= to =s6=) and mark =optional= items.

About the links, added on 2026-11-22:
- I assumed you buy from Denmark, so shops inside the EU come first. Say if that is wrong.
- Prices are as the shop showed them that day, in the shop's currency: Danish shops include VAT, and other EU shops mostly show their own country's VAT. For =EST= I converted at round rates of 6.5 kr and 0.87 euro to the dollar, which I did not look up.
- "Non-EU" means the parcel is imported: add 25% Danish VAT and a carrier's handling fee.
- Every link was opened by a script on 2026-11-22. One was dead and has been replaced; about one in ten answered with a bot check and could not be confirmed that way.
- Amazon.de and AliExpress entries were read through your own browser later the same day, with delivery set to Denmark and prices shown in kroner. Sellers there are mostly small marketplace traders, and their specifications are the sellers' own claims.
- Mouser entries come from its search API, read with your key by =tools/mouser.py=. Its prices are in kroner and, as far as I know, exclude VAT. Stock figures are from 2026-11-22.
- DigiKey, Farnell, RS and TME block automated reading, so they are thin here even though they stock many of the electronic parts.
- Stock and prices move. Treat each link as a starting point for comparison.

These totals are sums of the estimates below and assume you own none of
it. They leave out shipping, which ran from about $5 to $20 per shop
where I saw it, and import VAT on the non-EU items.

The store search raised every tier. My earlier figures ($150-180, $450,
$1,200 and $1,800) were guesses from memory. With real prices from shops
that deliver to Denmark, Stage 0 came out about 60% higher, the lean rig
more than double, and the full rig 40-60% higher. The biggest increases
are the cameras ($140 to $245) and the narrow-band LEDs ($85 to $280).
Amazon.de brought the pipettes, scale, tubes and pH pen back close to my
first guesses. Only Stage 0 fits the "$300+" budget you gave.

Ways to cut the lean rig: leave out the UV-A channel ($50), the spectral
sensors and light meter ($165), or use a webcam you already own ($60
each).

Section subtotals for the recommended picks: growing $305, light engine
$538, control and sensing $697, assay station $708, root-zone current
$93, tobacco $140.

replace most of the protoboard work. None is needed for Stage 0.

| Board           | Replaces                                 | Main parts                                                                                             |

I would prototype each on a breadboard through Stage 1 and order boards
once the circuit has stopped changing.
