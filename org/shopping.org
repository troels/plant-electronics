How to use this file:
- Each item is a heading. Change =BUY= to =ORDERED=, =HAVE= or =SKIP= as you go.
- =EST= is the estimated cost in US dollars for the whole quantity of the recommended pick.
- =PRICE= says whether that figure was =checked= by web search on 2026-11-22 or is from =memory=. Most are from memory, so check before ordering.
- =C-c C-x C-c= on a section shows it as a table with the costs summed.
- Tags give the stage that first needs the item (=s0= to =s5=) and mark =optional= items.

it. They are well above the "$300+" first-stage budget you gave, so the
lean rig is the natural first purchase. Things you likely have already
(a computer, a webcam, a bench supply, wire, a shelf) come straight off.

* Custom boards
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: custom-boards
:END:

Since you can design boards and have them made, three boards would
replace most of the protoboard work. None is needed for Stage 0.

| Board          | Replaces                                              | Main parts                                                     |
|----------------+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Zone controller | Dev board, MOSFET wiring, sensor cabling             | ESP32-S3 module, 5 MOSFET channels with gate drivers, 24 V input and buck converter, sensor connectors |
| Current source | Protoboard current source and monitors                | Four-channel DAC, op-amps, polarity switches, current sensing, isolated supply |
| Potentiostat   | Bought potentiostat, impedance breakout               | AD5941 or a discrete op-amp design, electrode-strip connector  |

I would prototype each on a breadboard through Stage 1 and order boards
once the circuit has stopped changing.
